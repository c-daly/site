Privacy policy

Last updated November 24, 2026

This policy covers visits to defiantduck.com and inquiries you send to Defiant Duck through the website or by email.

When you contact Defiant Duck

The inquiry form asks for your email address, a topic and a message. You can also give your name. Web3Forms delivers these details to Defiant Duck by email and may check your email and IP address for spam. Defiant Duck uses your message and contact details to respond and follow up on the work you ask about.

If you prefer to contact Defiant Duck directly, you can email {{email}}. Direct email bypasses Web3Forms.

How long inquiries are kept

Defiant Duck keeps inquiry emails in its inbox without a fixed deletion schedule. You can ask Defiant Duck to delete your correspondence.

Web3Forms has its own retention policy. It may keep a form submission for up to three years. A shorter period may apply depending on the plan or if the submission is deleted earlier. Web3Forms privacy policy

Website visits and advertising

GitHub Pages hosts this site. When you visit a page, GitHub logs your IP address to help keep the service secure. GitHub Pages data collection

Defiant Duck uses Google Analytics to see which pages people visit and Google Ads to measure which ads lead to inquiries. The site loads these services through Google Tag Manager. Your browser sends Google information such as the page URL and your IP address. Google can use cookies and other identifiers to connect activity on the site with an earlier ad click. How Google uses information from partner sites

When Web3Forms confirms an inquiry, the site reports the submission to Google Ads. Google’s tag may also convert the email address you enter into a code called a hash. Google can match that code with a Google account to determine whether an ad led to the inquiry. How Google uses enhanced conversion data

Your privacy choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser, manage personalized ads in Google My Ad Center, or limit Analytics collection with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

For privacy questions, or to request a copy, correction or deletion of information you shared with Defiant Duck, email {{email}}. Include the email address used for the inquiry so Defiant Duck can locate it.
